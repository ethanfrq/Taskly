View on GitHub Deploy your own

One sentence each morning.

Taskly never calls Claude and needs no API key. Your own Claude plan does the reading.

  1. Connect

    Add Taskly to Claude as a custom connector, once. You approve what it can do, and you can disconnect it in one tap.

  2. Ask

    Say “Prepare my day in Taskly”. Claude reads your mail, calendar and messages and adds what needs doing, each task linked to its source.

  3. Get reminded

    Taskly takes it from there: one clear checklist, and a notification at the right minute, even with Claude closed.

Open source. Yours to run.

MIT licensed and built in the open. Run your own instance on Vercel with Postgres, or on any Node.js server.
