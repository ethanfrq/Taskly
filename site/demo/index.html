Free and open source

Try Taskly, no account needed.

Everything runs in your browser: nothing is sent or saved. In the real app, your own Claude writes the tasks from your mail, calendar and messages.

  2. 2

    Or undo what Claude added, in one tap.

  3. 3

    Add a task in one sentence

    Try “Call Paul tomorrow 2pm”.

  4. 4

    Close the day

    The evening review moves what's left to tomorrow.
